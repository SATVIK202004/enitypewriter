How to Use

1

Download & Run the .exe

Download ENI_Typewriter.exe from the Releases section. Double-click to run. A window appears with a QR code and a URL. Your laptop is now a server.

2

Connect Your Phone

Make sure your phone is on the same Wi-Fi as your laptop. Open your phone camera, scan the QR code on the laptop screen, and tap the link. Or type the URL manually into Chrome/Safari.

3

Add to Home Screen (App Install)

In Chrome: tap ⋮ → "Add to Home Screen". In Safari: tap Share → "Add to Home Screen". Name it "Typewriter". Now it opens full-screen — no browser bars, just the app.

4

Paste Code & Type

Copy any code to your phone clipboard. Open the Typewriter app. Tap the text area — it auto-pastes. Click into your exam editor on the laptop. Tap ⚡ TYPE IT. Wait 2.5 seconds. Code types itself at 85 WPM.

5

STOP Anytime

Something goes wrong mid-type? Tap the red ■ STOP button. Typing halts instantly — even during the 2.5s grace period. The TYPE IT button reappears. Paste corrected code and retry.

6

Close When Done

After your exam, click Stop Server on the laptop window, or simply close it. The phone app disconnects. No traces. No logs.

Pro Tips

🎯

Webcam Choreography

Keep your phone on your lap. Raise it briefly to paste code (reads as "candidate holding chin while thinking"). The 2.5-second grace period gives you time to click back into the exam editor calmly.

🔄

Plagiarism Bypass

Change variable names as you paste — the AST checker won't match. The typing comes through as keyboard input, not copy-paste. Structurally unique code every time.

📶

Wi-Fi Only

The phone and laptop communicate over your local network. No internet needed. No data used. The connection never leaves your room.
